PROJECT 01 · SOLIDWORKS SIMULATION

Table Structural & Vibration Analysis

A finite element study evaluating how a table responds to four 200 lb loading conditions, followed by deformation and modal analyses. The work compares stress distribution at the narrow ends, center, and long edge, then examines the first three vibration modes.

  • Four Von Mises stress loading cases
  • Deformation review for the single-end load case
  • Modal frequencies: 35.9 Hz, 70.542 Hz, and 77.025 Hz
  • Communication of simulation results through annotated visuals
